14 [p] (*) 3.1. Capstone Project name: 
15 [p] 3.1.1. English: DroneOps Intelligence – An AI-Assisted Mission Planning, Monitoring, and Incident Analytics Platform for UAV Operations
16 [p] 3.1.2. Vietnamese: DroneOps Intelligence – Nền tảng hỗ trợ lập kế hoạch, giám sát và phân tích sự cố thông minh cho hoạt động UAV
17 [p]  (*) 3.2. Main proposal content (including result and product)   
18 [p] a) Context:
19 [p] UAV operations involve mission planning, pre-flight safety checks, telemetry monitoring, and post-flight incident analysis. In many small or experimental drone operations, these activities are fragmented across flight-control software, spreadsheets, maps, and manually reviewed logs.
20 [p] A mission-management platform can improve traceability and operational safety by validating flight plans before execution, ingesting standardized UAV telemetry, detecting abnormal events, and using historical missions to support risk assessment.
21 [p] DroneOps Intelligence focuses on the software platform around UAV operations rather than development of the low-level flight controller.
22 [p] 
23 [p] b) Proposed Solutions:
24 [p] Manage UAVs, pilots/operators, missions, waypoints, mission versions, and flight histories.
25 [p] Provide pre-flight Geofencing & Flight Path Validation.
26 [p] Check mission waypoints/segments against configured No-Fly Zones, restricted areas, and altitude limits before approval.
27 [p] Ingest standardized UAV telemetry using MAVLink, bridged to platform services through WebSocket or MQTT.
28 [p] Monitor location, altitude, speed, battery, connectivity, and mission progress in real time.
29 [p] Detect and record operational incidents and abnormal telemetry.
30 [p] Implement an AI Decision-Support service using similarity retrieval (k-NN/vector search) to retrieve historical missions similar to a proposed/current mission.
31 [p] Explain risk recommendations using evidence from Top-k similar missions and their incident histories rather than only providing an opaque risk score.
32 [p] Provide mission dashboards, incident analytics, and post-flight reports.
33 [p] 
34 [p] c) Functional Requirements:
35 [p] UAV Operator / Pilot:
36 [p] Register and manage mission plans.
37 [p] Define routes using waypoints.
38 [p] Submit missions for pre-flight validation.
39 [p] View geofence, restricted-zone, and altitude violations.
40 [p] Monitor UAV telemetry and mission progress.
41 [p] Receive operational alerts.
42 [p] Review post-flight mission and incident reports.
43 [p] Operations Manager:
44 [p] Manage UAVs, operators, mission approval policies, and operational areas.
45 [p] Define/import No-Fly Zones, geofences, and altitude restrictions.
46 [p] Review and approve/reject mission plans.
47 [p] Monitor active missions.
48 [p] Review incident histories and risk evidence.
49 [p] View operational analytics.
50 [p] Telemetry & Mission Service:
51 [p] Receive MAVLink-based telemetry through an integration gateway.
52 [p] Normalize and persist telemetry.
53 [p] Track UAV and mission state.
54 [p] Detect communication loss, low battery, abnormal altitude/speed, and configurable operational events.
55 [p] Maintain mission and incident histories.
56 [p] Flight Path Validation Service:
57 [p] Validate waypoints and route segments against geofences/No-Fly Zones.
58 [p] Validate altitude constraints.
59 [p] Identify violating route segments and provide evidence on a map.
60 [p] Prevent approval where mandatory safety constraints are violated.
61 [p] AI Decision-Support Service:
62 [p] Construct feature representations for historical and proposed missions.
63 [p] Retrieve Top-k similar historical missions using k-NN/vector search.
64 [p] Present similarity, relevant mission characteristics, and historical incidents.
65 [p] 
66 [p] d) Non-Functional Requirements:
67 [p] Interoperability: MAVLink shall be supported as the UAV telemetry standard at the integration boundary.
68 [p] Traceability: Mission plan versions, validation results, approvals, telemetry, and incidents must be auditable.
69 [p] Latency: Real-time telemetry and critical alerts should be delivered with operationally appropriate latency.
70 [p] Reliability: Temporary telemetry interruptions must not corrupt mission state.
71 [p] Security: Authentication, authorization, secure API communication, and protected mission data are required.
72 [p] Explainability: AI decision support must show historical evidence supporting similarity/risk recommendations.
73 [p] Maintainability: Telemetry adapters, validation rules, and analytics services should be modular.

107 [p] 3.3. Research Information
108 [p] a. Research Problem
109 [p] RQ1: How effectively can automated geofence and flight-path validation detect spatial and altitude constraint violations in UAV mission plans?
110 [p] RQ2: How accurately and efficiently can the platform ingest and process MAVLink telemetry for real-time mission monitoring and incident detection?
111 [p] RQ3: Can similarity-based retrieval of historical missions provide relevant and explainable evidence for UAV mission risk assessment?
112 [p] b. Research Objectives
113 [p] Develop a software platform for UAV mission planning, monitoring, and incident management.
114 [p] Develop and evaluate automated flight-path validation.
115 [p] Integrate MAVLink as a standardized telemetry interface.
116 [p] Develop and evaluate evidence-based similarity retrieval for mission decision support.
117 [p] c. Research Scope & Methodology
118 [p] The project focuses on mission operations software; low-level flight-control algorithm development is outside scope.
119 [p] Evaluation shall include:
120 [p] Geofence/path violation detection accuracy.
121 [p] Telemetry ingestion throughput and latency.
122 [p] Incident detection performance for defined conditions.
123 [p] Top-k retrieval relevance using Precision@k/Recall@k or expert relevance assessment.
124 [p] End-to-end mission workflow demonstrations.
125 [p] d. Expected Scientific Contribution
126 [p] An integrated architecture combining UAV mission validation, standardized telemetry, and incident analytics.
127 [p] An evaluated geospatial flight-path validation approach.
128 [p] An evidence-based historical mission retrieval method for explainable UAV operational decision support.
