9 [p] 2. System at a Glance
10 [p] DroneOps Intelligence is a Web mission-operations platform for UAV operators and operations managers. It centralizes aircraft, operator, mission and restriction data; validates planned routes before approval; ingests MAVLink-derived telemetry; detects and manages incidents; and preserves post-flight evidence. Operators plan and monitor missions, while managers review proposals, restrictions and operational outcomes. Advisory AI summarizes authorized context and suggests risks and precautions without approving missions or controlling a UAV. The core value is a traceable workflow from planning through approval, monitoring and post-flight review, replacing fragmented maps, logs and spreadsheets. System structure and trust boundaries are shown in Report 4, SDD §1.1, Figures 1–2.
