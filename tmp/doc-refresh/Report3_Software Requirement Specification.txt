35 [p] II. Software Requirement Specification
36 [p] 1. Product Overview
37 [p] DroneOps Intelligence manages UAVs, pilot accounts and flight credentials, mission planning and reviewed updates, configured flight restrictions, live telemetry, incidents, post-flight evidence and historical-mission-based decision support. The Web application supports Operators and Operations Managers. The Operator mobile client covers account/profile access, own/assigned mission viewing, validation and decision results, live monitoring, alerts and history; mission route editing and Manager administration are Web functions.
38 [p] The platform validates routes against configured restrictions before Manager approval. A MAVLink-compatible UAV/autopilot or simulator supplies telemetry through an integration gateway. The platform detects configured abnormal conditions, retains evidence and generates post-flight reports. AI decision support retrieves similar historical flights and their incident evidence; a configured OpenAI or Gemini service may summarize that evidence. AI advice never replaces mandatory validation or Manager decisions.
39 [p] Scope exclusions: no low-level navigation, stabilization, autonomous flight execution or replacement of GCS/autopilot software. Custom model training and autonomous AI approval are outside scope. Restriction checks cover configured data only; they do not certify compliance with unprovided restrictions.

72 [p] 3.1.3 Screen Authorization

Read access does not grant permission to modify evidence.

93 [p] 3.1.7 Lifecycle Diagrams
94 [p] A new mission begins in Draft. Initial submission triggers validation and Manager review. Every subsequent accepted pre-flight edit creates a pending update for the same mission and automatically triggers validation and review. The current mission remains unchanged while review is pending. The lifecycle and concurrency rules are defined in BR-13 and CR-05.

96 [p] Figure 13. Mission lifecycle.
97 [p] 
98 [p] Figure 14. Pending update request lifecycle.
99 [p] Approval atomically applies proposed content to the same mission ID. Rejecting an update preserves current content and operational status, including an existing Approved status. A mission with a pending update cannot begin execution. Cancellation affects the planning record and never sends a flight-control command.

248 [p] 5.5 Terms and Data Conventions
249 [p] Mission: the current planning record with one stable ID. Mission update request: a separately stored proposed change requiring review, not an executable mission or mission version. Flight: the immutable execution record created from an approved mission at start.
250 [p] MSL: altitude above mean sea level. AGL: altitude above local ground; conversion requires terrain/reference data. WGS84: geographic coordinate reference for latitude and longitude.
251 [p] Fresh telemetry: valid mapped data received within the configured communication-loss threshold. Stale/offline: the threshold has elapsed; last-known values remain visible with timestamps. An incident episode persists until measured recovery.
252 [p] Top-k retrieval: selecting up to k relevant completed flights using a recorded feature representation and similarity method. Similarity is not a calibrated probability of safety or incident occurrence.
